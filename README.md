# Dewey
    Dewey is an AI note taking application that records your meetings and provides an transcript for viewing and editing. The transcript is speaker direized and a synposis and the transcirpt can be linked and inserted into existing google documents.

# Features
- Meeting Recording
    Dewey will record your meetings for you and you have the option to keep the recording saved on your computer or deleted when Dewey has finished transcribing

- Full Transciption
    Dewey will provide a full transcription with speaker direization from the transcript that is derived from the meeting recording. You will be able to look over and make edits to the transcript from Dewey! Dewey will also provide a synopsis of the recording that can be edited as well.

- Google Docs integration
    Once you have approved the transcript and synopsis, you can connect a google account to select a google document to insert them both into the document.
